Send attachments, copies and a reply-to address per email. `EmailService.send` takes `cc`, `bcc`, `replyTo` (default: the module's configured `replyTo`) and `attachments`. An attachment is either `{ filename, contentType, content }`, whose bytes are stored with the queued email until it is sent or failed, or `{ filename, contentType, size, key }`, for which only that metadata is stored and the host's new `AttachmentResolver` service returns the bytes at every delivery attempt. A retry sends the same copies, reply-to and attachments; a resolver failure, or bytes of another length than `size`, fails that attempt like a provider error. Attachments of one email may add up to `maxAttachmentsBytes` (10 MiB); a larger send fails with the typed `AttachmentsTooLarge` (HTTP 413) and queues nothing. `OutgoingEmail` gains `cc`, `bcc` and `attachments`, so every `Mailer`, `captureMailer()` included, receives them; `Mailer.resend` sends them to Resend. The email log API and screen show cc, bcc, reply-to and each attachment's name and size, never its content. Migration `0003_add_email_log_copies_and_attachments` adds the `cc`, `bcc`, `reply_to` and `attachments` columns to `emails.email_log`.
