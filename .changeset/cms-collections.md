'@opulonjs/cms': minor
---

Add collections, site settings and menus. `defineCms` now takes `collections` (fields, optional `routed` for items with their own path, availability and SEO per content locale, optional `titleField`), `settings` (host fields added to the built-in global SEO fields `siteName`, `titleTemplate`, `defaultImage`, `twitterHandle`) and `menus` (by key, nesting `depth` 1 to 3). New field kinds: `fields.link` (a page, an item, a system page or a URL, stored as the reference) and `fields.reference({ collection, many })`. Items, the site settings and each menu hold a draft and a published document like pages, with preview, publish/unpublish, a revision per publish and Audit events (`cms.item.*`, `cms.settings.*`, `cms.menu.*`). Routed items share the URL space with pages, so a path change on publish leaves a redirect behind. Publishing purges `cms:item:<id>` + `cms:collection:<key>` (items) and `cms:site` (settings, menus). Admin screens under Content > Collections, Menus and Site settings; permissions `cms.collections.view|edit|publish` and `cms.site.view|edit|publish`. `@opulonjs/cms/server` exports `Items`, `Settings`, `Menus` and `resolveLinks(links, locale)` for the Read API.
